Point & Ask — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-17_

**What the extension does.** Point & Ask lets you circle part of a web page or PDF and ask a question about it.

Nothing is sent anywhere until you press **Ask** (or Enter) in the panel.

**What is sent when you ask.**

  • Your question.
  • The coordinates of the marks you drew and the size of your browser window.
  • The visible text of the page elements under your mark (and, in the PDF viewer, the text of the circled

    • region with page numbers), plus the page title and URL.

  • Depending on the privacy setting you chose on first use: an image crop of the marked region (default),

    • no image at all ("text only"), or the whole visible tab. You can change this any time from the extension icon.

  • A random device identifier generated on install (so you get a daily quota without an account), the

    • extension version, and, if you signed in, your session token.

    **What is never sent.** Keystrokes, browsing history, cookies, pages you did not ask about, anything from

    sites on the built-in blocklist (banking, payment, health portals, government) or from browser-internal pages.

    **Where it goes.** To the StudyOS server configured in the extension (by default the StudyOS service; self-hosters

    can point it at their own server). The server forwards the question, the text and the optional crop to the

    configured AI model provider to produce the answer, and stores the question, the text under the mark, and the

    answer in your history so you can continue in the tutor. Image crops are not stored. Anonymous history is kept

    for 30 days and then deleted unless you sign in, in which case it moves to your account.

    **Speech.** The microphone button uses your browser's built-in speech recognition by default (Chrome sends that

    audio to Google). Read-aloud uses your operating system's voice. Only if you enable **Power mode** does audio go

    to the configured server for transcription and synthesis.

    **Your controls.** Delete any mark from the StudyOS dashboard, sign out or uninstall the extension to remove the

    device identifier, and use the "text only" setting to keep all pixels on your machine.
